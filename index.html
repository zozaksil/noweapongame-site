001 / Arsenal

Candy gun

Everyone starts with it. Nobody runs out. It leaves your crew's colour on whoever's in the way.

002 / Arsenal

Toast double pop

Two slices, point blank. The closer you are, the more it hurts. Smells great on reload.

003 / Arsenal

Coke gun

Spray till they're stuck in a bubble. When it pops, they're sticky. Then they're yours.

004 / Arsenal

Boom box

Fires CDs. Cuts straight through people, bounces off walls. The DJ doesn't miss twice.

005 / Arsenal

Megaphone

Tap to shout. Hold, then let go — and their ears won't stop ringing.

006 / Arsenal

Cheese camera

Pull it out. Say cheese. Anyone close in the frame freezes mid-pose — and you keep the print.
